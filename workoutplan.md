# 💪 2-Month Lean Bulk Workout Plan

## Goal

- Build muscle over the next 2 months
- Keep workouts simple and easy to start
- Minimize injury risk
- Use mostly machines and dumbbells
- Flexible schedule (typically every other day, ~4 workouts/week)
- Progressive overload and consistent tracking

---

# General Principles

- 4 workouts per week (approximately every other day)
- 45–65 minutes per workout
- 5–6 exercises per workout
- Mostly machines and dumbbells
- Avoid heavy barbell exercises
- Focus on consistency rather than perfection

---

# Warm-up (Every Workout)

Since I already jog to the gym (~7 minutes), no additional cardio is needed.

Complete the following activation routine before lifting:

- Bird Dog — 10 reps per side
- Dead Bug — 10 reps per side
- Plank — 30–45 seconds
- Air Squats — 10–15 reps

Purpose:
- Activate core
- Activate lower back
- Prepare hips and shoulders
- Avoid fatigue before lifting

# Progressive Overload

Always stay within the target rep range.

Example:

Week 1

30 kg

- 8
- 8
- 7

Week 2

30 kg

- 10
- 10
- 9

Week 3

Increase weight

32.5 kg

- 8
- 7
- 6

Rule:

> Once all sets reach the upper end of the rep range with good form, increase the weight next session.

# Alternating Variants (Optional)

To keep things fresh across the 2 months while preserving the same per-muscle volume, you can rotate exercise variants week-to-week as long as sets/reps stay matched to the same muscle and rep range, e.g.:

- Swap DB Bench Press ↔ Incline DB Bench Press as the "first" exercise
- Swap Lat Pulldown ↔ Pull-ups as primary back-width move
- Swap Leg Press ↔ Bulgarian Split Squat emphasis within Workout C/D

This is optional — consistency with the plan above is more important than variation.

# Nutrition

- Eat in a slight calorie surplus (+200–300 kcal/day)
- Protein: **120–160 g/day**
- Sleep: **7–9 hours/night**
- Stay consistent rather than chasing the perfect workout.

# Long-Term Plan

Eventually, this plan can become dynamic.

Before each workout, simply decide:

- How much time do I have?
- Which muscles have I trained most recently?

Track for every exercise:

- Weight
- Sets
- Reps
- Date

Then generate each workout by prioritizing muscle groups that haven't been trained recently while continuing progressive overload, and keep total weekly sets per muscle aligned with the volume targets above.

This keeps the schedule flexible while ensuring balanced muscle growth.
